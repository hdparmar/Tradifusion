Tradi-fusion

Riffusion fine-tuned on Irish traditional music

My master's thesis at KTH, supervised by Bob L. T. Sturm. Riffusion is Stable Diffusion taught to draw music as spectrograms. I fine-tuned it on about 9,000 spectrograms cut from recordings of Irish traditional music, then asked it for jigs, reels and airs.

Everything below was generated in October 2026 with the published model and the pipeline in this repository: %%CLIPS%% clips of 5.12 seconds, all of them, unedited. The settings for each are in experiments.json.
